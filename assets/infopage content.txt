App Info Page Content: The Legacy Scorecard of Professor Isa Ali Pantami
1. Biography & Leadership Profile
●	Full Name & Titles: Professor Isa Ali Ibrahim (Pantami), CON, PhD, FCIIS, FBCS, FNCS.
●	Key Appointments:
○	Director-General/CEO, National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) from 2016 to 2019.
○	Minister of Communications and Digital Economy of the Federal Republic of Nigeria from 2019 to 2023 (serving as Nigeria's first-ever Minister of Digital Economy).
●	Global Leadership: Appointed by the United Nations through the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) as the Chairman of the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) in 2022, chairing a council of over 193 global ministers.
●	Traditional Honor: Conferred with the title Majidadin Daular Usmaniyya (The Glorious One of the Othman ibn Fodiyo Empire) by the Sultan of Sokoto for his philanthropic work and public service.
2. Key National Impacts & Statistics
●	Macroeconomic Growth: Guided the ICT sector to contribute an unprecedented 18.44% to Nigeria’s GDP, making it the fastest-growing sector of the economy in 2020.
●	Revenue & Cost Optimization: Achieved a 594% increase in government revenue generation by 2022 while concurrently reducing broadband and product pricing by over 60%.
●	Legislative Milestones: Masterminded the development of the landmark Nigeria Start-Up Act, 2022 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.
●	Total Projects Executed: Designed and executed a record-breaking 4,366 projects and programmes nationally during his four-year ministerial tenure.
3. Regional Focus: North-East Zonal Headquarters (Gombe State)
Under Professor Pantami's leadership, Gombe State was strategically designated as the North-East regional headquarters for crucial federal ICT infrastructure due to its central location and cosmopolitan environment. Major zonal installations include:
●	NITDA North-East Zonal Office: Completed in 2022 (Project Cost: N318,169,671.00).
●	NCC Permanent Zonal Office: Completed in 2022 adjacent to Flossy Hospital (Project Cost: N397,182,887.92).
●	Galaxy Backbone (GBB) Zonal Office: Completed in 2021 as a hub for regional marketing and commercialization (Project Cost: N408,383,229.78).
●	NIGCOMSAT Regional Office: Commissioned in October 2020 to bring satellite-driven digital solutions to the zone.
4. Major Transformative Infrastructure
Broadbnd Infrastructure Projects
A comprehensive rollout of high-speed wireless internet broadband hotspots across markets, higher education institutions, and public venues:
●	NICTIB II (Information Communications Technology Infrastructure Backbone): Facilitated an 1,866 KM 48-Core fiber optic cable network running from Kano through Potiskum, Gombe, and adjacent regions, valued at approximately N3 billion for the Gombe State segment alone.
●	Commercial Hubs: Broadband deployments at the New Market Gombe (N338,238,241.90) and the Gombe Main Market server room.
●	Airports: High-speed internet deployment at the Gombe Lawanti International Airport (N222,038,025.00).
