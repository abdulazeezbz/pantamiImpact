Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy details how the Pantami Impact Tracker mobile & web application collects, uses, and safeguards information.

Effective Date: August 6, 2026 Google Play & AdMob Compliant

1

Overview & Application Purpose

Pantami Impact Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") is an informational platform created to showcase, track, and locate 242+ federal infrastructure, ICT, and CSR projects facilitated across Gombe State and Nigeria.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pantami Impact Tracker mobile application (iOS & Android) and web application. By accessing or using our application, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

We do not require users to register an account, enter passwords, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your full name, home address, or phone number) to browse project listings.

Automatically Collected Technical Data:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Log & Performance Data: Application crash reports, performance metrics, and feature usage analytics.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID or IDFA) used strictly for ad delivery and frequency capping.

4

Maps & Location Data

The App features an interactive project map powered by OpenStreetMap (Nominatim API).

  • Location queries are sent to geocode project addresses (e.g., "Kashere", "Gombe Bypass").
  • We do not track, store, or share your continuous real-time GPS movements on our servers.
5

Data Retention & Security

Project images and data are fetched over secure HTTPS encrypted connections (`https://abzdatasub.com.ng/pantami`). Cached images stored locally on your device can be cleared at any time through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

6

Children's Privacy

Our App is an educational and public information resource designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7

Your Choices & Ad Opt-Out

You have the right to disable personalized ads on your device:

  • Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization or Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Toggle off tracking permissions.
